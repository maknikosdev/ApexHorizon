# Apex Horizon — Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 25 November 2026_

Apex Horizon ("the app") is a driving-awareness app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## Summary

- The app uses the internet **only** to download the offline map files you choose (plain HTTPS downloads of a small map list and map files). Your location, destinations, routes and device details are never sent anywhere.
- The app has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs** that collect data.
- Everything the app processes stays **on your device**.

## What the app uses and why

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Precise location (GPS) | Show your speed, direction and predict the road ahead. Used **only while a drive screen is open**; the app never requests background location. | Processed on the device. If you choose "Save to history", the track of that run is stored in the app's private storage. |
| Motion sensors (gyroscope, accelerometer) | Smooth the speed read-out and detect braking and cornering. | Processed on the device, not stored except as summary values of a saved run. |
| Camera (optional) | Show the live road under the HUD and measure overall brightness to estimate visibility. | Frames are analysed in memory for average brightness only. **Images and video are never saved or transmitted.** |
| GPX files you import | Provide road geometry for curve distances. | Copied (as coordinates only) into the app's private storage on your device. |

## Retention and deletion

Saved runs and the imported route remain on your device until you delete them in the app (History → delete, or Settings → "Delete all saved runs"), or until you uninstall the app, which removes all app data.

## Sharing

The app does not share data with anyone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Safety notice

Apex Horizon is an informational aid, not a navigation or safety system. Suggested speeds are estimates. Always follow traffic laws and your own judgement, and do not interact with your phone while driving.
