# Play Store listing text (English)

**App name:** Apex Horizon

**Short description (max 80 chars):**
See the road before it arrives: calm speed HUD and curve-ahead guidance.

**Full description:**
Apex Horizon turns your phone into a calm, glanceable driving HUD.

• Smooth speed read-out — GPS fused with your phone's motion sensors, so the number never jumps nervously.
• Road-ahead prediction — a glowing path curves ahead of you and marks the apex of the next bend.
• Curve guidance — "Left curve in 180 m • Suggested approach ~60 km/h" when you import a GPX route.
• Works with or without the camera — with it, the HUD floats over the live road; without it, the same HUD is drawn on a synthetic horizon.
• Honest when conditions are bad — in low light or weak GPS the confidence score drops and the app tells you it is estimating instead of inventing a line.
• Trip summary — distance, duration, average and maximum speed, and a timeline of firm braking and intense corners. Save runs and replay them.
• Demo mode — try everything instantly, no driving or permissions needed.

Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Plan trips with free offline OpenStreetMap maps; your location and destinations never leave your device.

Apex Horizon is an informational aid, not a navigation or safety system. Mount your phone securely, never interact with it while driving, and always follow traffic laws.
